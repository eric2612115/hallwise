# Hallwise living-room take, v4 lines

The whole take in reading order, from the friend's door to Kenji's murmur. Every guest speaks English to Eric,
in their own accent; only two short asides, said to themselves, are in French and Japanese. Eric's and
[FRIEND]'s lines are **LIVE**: spoken in the room, never played. They are here so they can be rehearsed
against `rehearsal-mix.mp3`, which leaves a silence for each one (its words / 2.6 per second + 0.8 s).

Guest durations are measured from the rendered speech; LIVE durations are estimates (words / 2.6 per second).
The words in square brackets are Eleven v4 audio tags (how the line is performed), not spoken. Every guest line
was also rendered with its accent tag in front (see Cast), left out of the lines below.

## Beats the app needs, and where they are

- **Leo Wong, Bright Path**: name and company in his first line; Eric's deck promise ("I'll send you our deck
  tonight") is LIVE right after, and Leo repeats "Tonight?".
- **The French aside** comes in the sofa chat, before anyone has a name, so the morning draft can quote what
  Margaux said "before anyone knew her name" (SCRIPT.md, The payoff). It is whispered, then lifted to the level
  of her other lines, with about 0.9 s of pause on each side; Kenji's murmur is treated the same way. Played
  alone, both asides transcribe exactly; inside the full take, at a whisper's own level and tight against the
  next line, the first pipeline run lost both.
- **Margaux**: name and company, pre-seed this quarter, a one-pager with numbers (said in English as well, so the
  promise is clear). Chloé is left implicit, as SCRIPT.md has it: the app links Margaux to Chloé (Corvane
  Capital, Blockchain Week, Oct 23) from the people index, which is the point of the beat.
- **Julien**: "Julien. Julien Caron, also Corvane": his first name twice and his company once, since his
  surname never came through in the Kokoro rehearsals. The demo invite: "Wednesday afternoon?"
- **The waiter**: one line, small talk the app sets aside.
- **Siddharth Raghunathan, Quill Mesh**: full name with company once (the app matches first name + company).
- **Kenji**: greets first ("We keep running into each other"), Eric fumbles, Kenji helps with name, company and
  where they met. Then "Thursday is... no, Friday. Friday is better." and the murmur 金曜日ね、メモしておこう。
- **Spacing**: two introductions in one cue are held at least 1.6 s apart (Margaux/Julien,
  Siddharth/Kenji); every guest line is followed by at least 1.5 s before the next cue (a LIVE line or a walk
  sits between every two cues; the waiter and Siddharth are 3 s apart).
